02 / Evolution, not mythology

How genes travel.

Phylogenetic studies point to inheritance and possible horizontal gene transfer. These explanations teach the distinction, not the exact historical routes.

↧

Vertical inheritance

Parent fungal lineages pass genetic material to descendants. Some branches preserve a biosynthetic gene cluster; others may differ.

⇢

Horizontal gene transfer

Genetic material may transfer between lineages outside ordinary descent. Scientific studies infer such transfers for psilocybin-related genes.

◌

What remains unknown

No exact ancient individual organism or complete sequence of events has been directly observed. Evolutionary inference comes with uncertainty.
