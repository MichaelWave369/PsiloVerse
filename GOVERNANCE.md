# PsiloVerse scientific governance

**IDENTITY ≠ OCCURRENCE ≠ GENETIC CAPACITY ≠ EXPRESSION ≠ METABOLITE DETECTION ≠ HUMAN EFFECT ≠ SAFETY**

1. Every research claim requires a stable ID, research lane, source reference, status, and limitations.
2. Peer-reviewed literature can support a claim; the UI cannot strengthen that claim.
3. A molecular-clock estimate is not a directly observed specimen date; a reconstructed horizontal gene transfer is not a witnessed ancient event.
4. Genus-level statements never establish the chemistry or safety of every species.
5. Predicted genes are not equivalent to measured metabolites, clinical benefits, or human effects.
6. Research hypotheses are kept in a separate notebook and never promoted automatically to confirmed scientific claims.
7. Religious symbolism and archaeological evidence remain distinct; there is no direct proof that Eden's trees encoded psilocybin mushrooms.
8. This atlas offers no wild mushroom identification, cultivation methods, extraction, synthesis, dosing, or medical treatment advice.
9. New evidence updates should be reviewable and preserve prior claim history.
10. Passing CI proves data-contract integrity, not independent replication of all cited science.

## Scientific status labels

- `PUBLISHED_EXPERIMENT`: Source-reported laboratory work
- `PUBLISHED_STUDY_METHOD`: Source-reported research method/sample
- `GENOMIC_INFERENCE`: Interpretations from genome or comparative analysis
- `PHYLOGENOMIC_INFERENCE`: Model-dependent ancestry/dating
- `GENOMIC_HYPOTHESIS`: Hypothesis supported by genomic analysis
- `EVOLUTIONARY_HYPOTHESIS`: Inferred evolutionary mechanism
- `METHOD_BOUNDARY`: Explicit limit on what data can establish

Hypothesis notebook labels include `SPECULATIVE_NO_DIRECT_EVIDENCE`, `CONTRADICTED_AS_RECENT_SHARED_SPECIES`, and `PLAUSIBLE_GENERAL_EVOLUTION_UNQUANTIFIED`.
