# PsiloVerse v0.1: methods and limitations

## Scope

PsiloVerse is an educational, manually curated source-bounded atlas, not a comprehensive review, laboratory experiment, or validated reconstruction of ancient organisms. Nine claims refer to three published studies, while three speculative questions occupy a visibly separate notebook. The static frontend renders `data/atlas.json`; it cannot create scientific authority.

## Evidence anchors

- Bradshaw et al. (2024), https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2311245121. The approximately 67-million-year estimate refers to the **stem lineage** of `Psilocybe` and is a molecular dating inference, not a recovered ancient fungus. The paper describes evolutionary relationships, gene-cluster organization and possible horizontal transfers.
- Fricke et al. (2017), https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201705489. Reports characterization of psilocybin biosynthetic enzymes. This does not mean every fungus carrying related genes produces predictable metabolite levels.
- Liu et al. (2025), https://doi.org/10.5943/mycosphere/16/1/28. Discusses gene-cluster evolution and study-specific horizontal transfer hypotheses; details depend on sampling and models.

## Cannabiverse relationship

Cannabis is a flowering plant. `Psilocybe` is a fungal genus. Their shared eukaryotic ancestry is immensely older than the diversification of cannabis cultivars or psilocybin-producing fungal lineages. The projects may share interface conventions, but molecular identity, genetic capacity, and proven biochemical occurrence must remain distinct.

## Eden hypothesis notebook

The Eden hypothesis is recorded to ask what evidence would be needed, not to present symbolism as confirmed ancient botany. None of the current sources establishes that the Tree of Knowledge represented psilocybin or that the Tree of Life represented cannabis. The notebook presents competing interpretations and tests.

## Prospective simulator

A later optional educational evolution module could explore hypothetical lineage branching, mutation, and horizontal transfer with deterministic seeds. It would need explicit artificial-model labels and would be barred from updating factual gene graphs. **This version does not implement an evolutionary simulator.**

## Safety and limits

No identification guidance, cultivation protocol, extraction methods, dosage guidance, synthesis details, or clinical claims are offered here.
